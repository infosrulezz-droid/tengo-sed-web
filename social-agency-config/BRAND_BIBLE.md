## 🗣️ Voice & Tone Guidelines
- **Primary Tone:** Charismatic, authoritative, and modern.
- **Secondary Tone:** Slightly mysterious, high-status, and punchy.
- **The "Vibe":** Minimalist but impactful. Less is more.

## ✅ The "Winning" Patterns (Do This)
- **The Pattern Interrupt:** Start with a bold claim or a surprising question.
- **Short-form Logic:** Use 1-3 word sentences to create rhythm.
- **Visual Synergy:** Use emojis that match the brand color palette (e.g., 💎, 🚀, ⚡).
- **The Bridge:** Always connect the trending topic back to the product catalog.

## ❌ The "AI-isms" (NEVER Use These)
- "In a world where..."
- "Unlock your potential..."
- "Dive deep into..."
- "Revolutionize your..."
- "Harness the power of..."
- Avoid generic "corporate" adjectives. Use "sharp," "cold," "elite," "raw."
